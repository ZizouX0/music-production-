How to use it

One topic per page, built for mid-session glances. Gold chips = first grab. Green chips = Ableton stock (always first per the three-option rule). Plain chips = owned alternatives for specific flavors. The repo markdown stays the source of truth — regenerate after updates.

The whole system in one line: calibrate the foundation with meters → balance with faders → fix only what's broken → season with character tools → verify against a reference, then bounce at −6 dBFS.

Session Setup

12 steps · before any fader moves
1

Same track order, every project. Drums → bass → synths → vocals → FX.

2

Name + color-code consistently. Your hands learn the map.

3

Delete dead tracks; park "maybes" in a muted _PARKED group.

4

Group to busses: DRUMS · BASS · SYNTHS · VOCALS · FX (Cmd+G).

5

Gain stage every channel to peak between −30 and −6 dBFS — clip gain / Utility, never the fader.

6

All faders at 0. Master at 0. Forever.

7

10 ms fades on every audio clip edge (Cmd+Opt+F). Kills clicks.

8

Tempo + signature set, section locators dropped (intro / groove / break / drop / outro).

9

Buffer 1024 while mixing. Latency is free; crackles are not.

10

REF track: 2–3 pro tracks in your lane, muted, Utility at −6…−8 dB for fair A/B, sends off.

11

Meters last on Main: mvMeter2 (VU Standard) → Youlean. Nothing after them.

12

Save as Template once — setup becomes 2 minutes per track.

Why it matters: every step converts a recurring cost (searching, re-deciding, recalibrating) into a one-time cost. Pros aren't faster because of secret tricks — their attention is free for the ten decisions that matter.

The Foundation

Build these three first · then never touch
1

KICK alone → Youlean True Peak Max ≈ −5.0 dBFS. Reset ✕ → play 8 bars → read → adjust Utility gain → repeat. Shape (tune in Simpler, tail, clip) BEFORE calibrating. The kick never moves again.

2

+ BASS → VU needle bounces into 0…+3 (red zone) on the groove — breathing, not pinned. Tune to kick root · sidechain under kick (stock Comp or Kickstart 2, ~4 dB) · sub mono <120 Hz.

3

+ CLAP → solo True Peak −6…−5 (ceiling, not goal). In context: heard clearly, kick stays boss. Summed peak on 2/4 stays ≈ −4 or lower.

4

STRESS TEST: trio only → stock Limiter on Main → push to −9…−11 LUFS Short-Term. Listen 30 s: kick still punches? Nothing crushed? Watch PSR: healthy ≈ 9–10 LU, squashed ≤ 6.

5

Fix what failed, re-test, then DELETE the limiter. It was only a test. Foundation locked — every later problem is, by elimination, something you added after.

If the test fails — who's guilty

You hearCauseFix
Kick lost its thump / pumpingBass energy eating the limiterLess bass sustain · deeper sidechain · bass down
Dirt / distortion on bass notesToo much sub for the loudnessBass level down · tame lowest octave
Clap vanishedAll transient, no body+250 Hz body — not more volume
Rule: solo = calibration, context = decision. The kick is the only element fully set in solo.

Needle bounces into red on hits, falls back between = correct. Pinned solid in red = low end too loud or over-compressed. Needle = average energy (what big systems feel); peaks = ceiling only.

Habits that keep numbers honest

• ✕ reset before every measurement — True Peak Max holds forever until reset, including your old, louder mistakes.
• Meters sit last on Main; mvMeter2 pre-gain stays at 0 there.
• True Peak (Youlean) over Live's sample-peak number — inter-sample peaks read ~0.3–1 dB higher and they're the truth.
• SPAN = spectrum shape vs. reference · REFERENCE = level-matched A/B · Tonal Balance 2 = genre curve check.

Mindset: meters set the skeleton once — then ears and the reference decide. Glance at the needle only when something feels off.

EQ System

One question: wrong — or nicer?

Something is WRONG → fix it

EQ Eight (stock), always. Sweep a narrow +8 dB bell, find the nasty spot, cut 3–6 dB. Mud lives 200–400 · boxy 500–800 · harsh 2–6k. HPF supporting parts ~100–200 Hz.

Problem comes & goes? → soothe2 (auto-finds harshness) or Pro-Q 4 dynamic bands / TDR Nova.

EQ Eight ⭐Pro-Q 4soothe2TDR Nova

Want it NICER → four buttons

Lows (kick/bass bigger AND cleaner) → Ozone Vintage EQ: boost + atten same freq.
Punch (too polite) → Solid EQ, boost where it lives.
Shine (dull top) → Maag Air Band +2–3.
Whole group warmer → Passive EQ (broad tube strokes).

Ozone Vintage EQSolid EQMaag EQ2Enhanced EQ

Saturation

All of it adds harmonics — ask: how much heat?

🔥 WARM — invisible thickness ("more expensive")

Busses & refinement. You hear it only on bypass. HG-2: Pentode/Triode = flavor, Density = drive both, level-compensated, + Air on drum bus.

HG-2UAD 610 / CenturySaturation KnobSDRR2 TubeLittleRadiatorSaturator · Soft Sine, low drive

🔥🔥 HOT — audible character ("more present")

The mixing level. Saturn 2 house trick: saturate bass MIDS only (150 Hz–1 kHz), sub stays clean → audible on phones, no mud.

Saturator ⭐Saturn 2 (multiband)Fresh Air (highs-only)FireChargerRC-20 / Vinyl (texture)

🔥🔥🔥 BURNING — the effect IS the sound ("more dangerous")

Roar = 3 stages, serial/parallel/M-S/multiband routing, tunable feedback, built-in comp + modulation. Growls, builds, destruction.

Roar ⭐Cyberdrive CoreElectrum / Bassment (amps)SausageFattener (cranked)

Reverb & Delay

Type → where → settings @ 124 BPM

Placement rule

Shared space, mixable wetness → return (100% wet, EQ before: HPF ~300). Reverb that becomes the sound (freeze, design) → insert. In doubt → return.

The two-return system (set once, in the template)

A "Ambience": small room 250–750 ms — everything sends a little; placement, not effect. B "Lead Verb": VintageVerb plate/hall 1.2–2.5 s, pre-delay ~60 ms, sidechain-ducked by the lead (comp after reverb, 3–6 dB, rel 200–400 ms) — big AND clear.

Mixing the Tracks

Lead first · faders before plugins
1

Lead in first: up-front = 1–2 dB above the clap; tucked = 1–2 dB below.

2

Everything else one at a time, faders only. Supporting parts 1 dB quieter than you think. This static balance is 70% of the mix.

3

Fix only what's broken (EQ page 06, comps page 07). Good after balance = done. Many tracks need nothing.

4

Reference check: level-matched; ask low end / lead level / brightness. Fix the biggest gap, stop after 2–3 rounds.

Common problems → exact move

ElementSymptomMove
KickNo low end / not forward+40–60 Hz or saturate · transient shaper for attack
Too long / hollowShorten tail (ShaperBox curve) · don't over-cut 150–250
ClapThin / buried+250 Hz body · brightness or saturation up top
Too dry / too wetPlate send · gate the tail
BassClashing with kickSidechain to kick · tune to root
Invisible on phonesSaturn 2 mids · boost mids, never sub
Lead/VocalHarshsoothe2 / Pro-DS · dip 5–10 kHz
BuriedCut 1–3 kHz in the masker — not more lead volume
AnythingMuddy mixCut 200–400 Hz in the competitors · HPF supports
Clarity comes from subtraction — mute or filter non-essentials before reaching for processing. The arrangement is the biggest EQ.

Drum Bus & Punch

Kick stays OUT of the bus

The chain (2–3 devices, not five)

1 · EQ Eight: HPF 100–120 (no kick in here) · tame 3–5k if harsh · tiny shelf +1–2 dB @ 8–10k if dull.
2 · Glue: 2:1, atk 10–30 ms, rel Auto, 1–2 dB GR. Bypass test: on = "a kit", off = "files".
3 · Color (optional): Drum Buss ⭐ (Drive + Crunch, Boom OFF, 30–50% wet) or HG-2 (Density + Air) / SausageFattener (low).
Groove first: velocities vary, 16th swing 10–20%, off-beat hats quieter — no plugin fixes a stiff pattern.

Transient tools — which kind

KindBehaviorTools
Curve/LFO (deterministic)Same shape every hit — peaks can't wobble. Tail shortening, pump.ShaperBox 3 ⭐ · Gatekeeper · LFOTool · Kickstart 2
Detector shapersReact to the hit — musical, can breathe on deep settings.Drum Buss ⭐ (stock) · Trinity Shaper (atk/body/sus, gentle!) · HelperTransients2 · SPL Attacker · Transient Master
One-knob punchInstant slap + saturation.UrbanPuncher
Clipper afterPins every hit to the same ceiling.KClip 3 · KNOCK · Saturator-clip ⭐

Printing through a group (commit moves)

New audio track → Audio From: group · Post Mixer → Monitor Off → arm → record (+2 bars for tails). Captures group FX + upstream automation; returns and downstream are NOT included. Freeze inside a group ignores group FX. Keep sources muted in _PARKED — the print is commitment, the source is the undo.

Mistakes: over-gluing (4+ dB kills hats) · kick inside a glued bus (undiagnosable pumping) · fixing one bad element on the whole bus.

Space, Width & Story

Where things sit · how the track moves

Panning — LCR, house edition

Center + mono, always: kick · bass/sub · lead · clap · vocal.
Sides: hats, shakers, percs, synth layers — hard L/R or halfway; pairs mirrored.
1–2 elements at half-pan fill the field. Width is contrast: wide works because the center is solid.

Width tools

Utility ⭐ (width %, mono maker <120 Hz) · Ozone Imager 2 · Wider · Wide Awake (Dada Life) · unlinked delay (p. 09). Mono check 10 s before every bounce — nothing important may vanish.

Movement & builds

Pump: Kickstart 2 ⭐ (one knob) · ShaperBox volume curve (drawn) · stock sidechain comp (transparent).
Filter motion: Auto Filter ⭐ · Volcano 3 · Supermodal.
Risers/FX: Endless Smile (one-knob build) · Eternal Return (riser) · RVRSE (reverse-verb riser) · Raum freeze · Movement (rhythmic texture).

The story (Phase 3 truth)

Mix in the arrangement, not the loop: automate send levels + filters into/out of sections · reverb throw on the last clap before the drop · drums dip in breaks · re-establish hierarchy when flat — the hook leads.

Contrast is energy: dry vs wet, bright vs dark, narrow vs wide between sections beats any single loud moment. Mixing is emotional storytelling with volume.
