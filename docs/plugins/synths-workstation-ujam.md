# Workstation, preset & UJAM instruments — verified reference

Sources: ujam.com + Usynth User Guide 1.4.1 (official PDF), support.ujam.com, unitedplugins.com, refx.com, kv331audio.com, u-he.com, korg.com, help.uaudio.com, splice.com / support.splice.com, xlnaudio.com, airmusictech.com (+ launch press for AIR Jura), venomode.com, klevgrand.com, Lunacy Audio launch materials, Analog Obsession (Patreon, via Bedroom Producers Blog coverage). Items that could not be confirmed on an official page are marked **(unverified)**.

> **Identification correction — read first.** The seven "...Core" binaries in the scan are **NOT UJAM Usynth editions**. They are **United Plugins** free/entry "Core" versions from the UnitedFreeBundle (sub-brands: Muramasa, JMG Sound, Instant Audio, Soundevice Digital). Likewise, **LOVEND is Analog Obsession** (not UJAM LO-FI anything) and **UrbanPuncher is United Plugins/Soundevice Digital** (not a UJAM Beatmaker). The genuine UJAM plugins in the scan are USYNTH, GM-ONE, FIN-MICRO, FIN-RETRO and VB-ROWDY.

## UJAM Usynth (binary: USYNTH)

**What it is:** UJAM's streamlined synth platform. A **single Usynth plug-in hosts all purchased "Titles" (editions)** — which explains the one `USYNTH` binary; the scan name alone does not reveal which Titles are licensed (check the Title switcher in the plugin, or the UJAM App). Current Titles (ujam.com, Oct 2026): 2080 (future-retro/synthwave), CARAMEL (warm pads/ambient), CORE (all-purpose), DELUXE (soulful keys), DRIVE (house — **free**), EUPHORIA (EDM leads), GLAM (pop), PIXEL (retro-game), TURBO-XT (90s romplers), FLUID (**melodic techno**), STRANGER (dark soundscapes), VORTEX (Y2K trance), WEBCORE (hyperpop), DUST (lo-fi). Engine (same across Titles): 2 layers per note, 1–7 oscillators per layer (virtual analog / wavetable / FM / multisample), multimode filter, two 5-stage envelopes + mod envelope, LFOs, 12-way mod matrix; sound-designer-wired macro knobs instead of raw synth parameters.

**Main controls** (verified, Usynth User Guide 1.4.1):
- **Synthesizer section:** preset selector, **Dark/Bright** hardware knob, **Fast/Slow** hardware knob, plus per-preset "software" (smart) knobs wired by the sound designers; Surprise bypass.
- **Sequencer** (sequencer/arpeggiator/phrase player): On/Off, Latch, Rate, Sync, preset selector; software knobs **Note Length, Swing, Octaves, Pattern Length**.
- **Finisher** (multi-effect, up to 100 effect units): On/Off, mode/preset selector, large **Effect** hardware knob, **Vari** knobs.
- **Surprise section:** Surprise button + Small/Big (variation amount) knob, snapshots — randomizes section settings.
- **Delay:** On/Off, Mix, presets, Rate, Feedback. **Ambience (reverb):** On/Off, Mix, presets.
- Volume + level meter, interactive keyboard with sequencer-control octave, PB/Mod. **Global:** Bend Range, Dynamic, Octave, Tune, Scale, Chord; MIDI learn & full automation.

**Use for house:** DRIVE (free) and FLUID are literally house/melodic-techno Title packs — fast stabs, arps and basses at 120–126 BPM with the Sequencer synced to Live.

**Manual:** https://www.ujam.com/fileadmin/products/Manuals/Usynth_User_Guide_1_4_1.pdf

## United Plugins "Core" series (free/entry versions of paid United Plugins effects)

All are effects, not instruments. Developer sub-brands verified from unitedplugins.com; each Core is a stripped-down build of the paid plugin of the same name. HyperspaceCore is permanently free; the others ship in the UnitedFreeBundle (regular $0/low-cost promos). Manuals/product pages: https://unitedplugins.com (per-product pages, e.g. https://unitedplugins.com/HyperspaceCore/).

### Bassment Core (binary: BassmentCore) — Muramasa / United Plugins
**What it is:** Lightweight bass-amp simulation; Core version of Bassment, the all-in-one bass amp & tone processor. **Use for house:** quick grit/weight on a DI bass layer — but stock Saturator + EQ Eight usually covers this. **Manual:** https://unitedplugins.com/Bassment/

### Cyberdrive Core (binary: CyberdriveCore) — JMG Sound / United Plugins
**What it is:** Multi-mode distortion; Core version of Cyberdrive (multi-stage, multi-mode, multi-FX distortion). **Use for house:** aggressive color on synth stabs/drum loops; stock fallback: Saturator/Roar. **Manual:** https://unitedplugins.com/Cyberdrive/

### Electrum Core (binary: ElectrumCore) — Muramasa / United Plugins
**What it is:** Stripped-down, low-CPU guitar amp + cabinet simulator; Core version of Electrum (amp/cab/pedal suite). **Use for house:** lo-fi amp color on keys or vocal chops; stock fallback: Amp/Cabinet. **Manual:** https://unitedplugins.com/Electrum/

### HyperspaceCore (binary: HyperspaceCore) — JMG Sound / United Plugins — FREE
**What it is:** Free compact algorithmic reverb built on the flagship Hyperspace algorithms (released June 2025, successor of Hyperspace CM). **Main controls:** XY pad morphing algorithm parameters (automatable), 3-level randomizer (subtle → experimental), wet/dry, tempo-syncable parameters, CPU sleep mode. **Use for house:** quick creative verb on percussion/FX; stock fallback: Hybrid Reverb. **Manual:** https://unitedplugins.com/HyperspaceCore/

### Orbitron Core (binary: OrbitronCore) — JMG Sound / United Plugins
**What it is:** Multi-mode modulator with six modulation types; Core version of Orbitron (quad morphing multi-mode modulator). **Use for house:** movement on pads/top loops; stock fallback: Auto Filter + LFO, Chorus-Ensemble, Phaser-Flanger. **Manual:** https://unitedplugins.com/Orbitron/

### QuickVox Core (binary: QuickVoxCore) — Instant Audio / United Plugins
**What it is:** Compact version of QuickVox, a vocal channel strip for fast vocal mixing. **Use for house:** one-stop polish on vocal chops; your fuller chain (Pro-Q 4 / Pro-C 2 / soothe2) outclasses it for lead vocals. **Manual:** https://unitedplugins.com/QuickVox/

### Retronaut Core (binary: RetronautCore) — JMG Sound / United Plugins
**What it is:** Vintage analog-style chorus/vibrato; Core version of Retronaut — adds analogue modulation, saturation and tape-style wow & flutter, with vibrato and four-voice chorus modes. **Use for house:** instant vintage width/wobble on melodic-house keys and pads; stock fallback: Chorus-Ensemble + Wavetable's drift, or your RC-20. **Manual:** https://unitedplugins.com/Retronaut/

## UJAM Groovemate ONE (binary: GM-ONE)

**What it is:** UJAM's percussion performer plug-in: real-time shaker, claps, tambourine and one-shot-shaker grooves (not a synth).

**Main controls** (official user guide): 30 Styles (Combi and Solo) built from the four sounds; patterns mapped from C3 with individual hits, **three pattern intensities and a Fill**; global playback: **Latch, Micro Timing, Player Sync, Swing**; **Mix** section with 9 Mix presets, **Reverb/Ambience** with 4 presets; automatable parameters, MIDI Learn, and **MIDI drag & drop** of phrases into the DAW.

**Use for house:** instant humanized shaker/clap top-loops at 120–126 BPM — drag the MIDI out and reskin with your own drum rack samples.

**Manual:** https://support.ujam.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021216580-User-Guide-Groovemate-ONE

## UJAM Finisher MICRO (binary: FIN-MICRO) — FREE

**What it is:** Free single-knob multi-effect from UJAM's Finisher series.

**Main controls** (official FIN-MICRO user guide): **25 Modes** (each a pre-designed combination of effects) and one **Amount** macro knob, multi-wired into the engine with a custom per-mode assignment. That is the whole interface (no Vari knobs in MICRO).

**Use for house:** one-knob transition FX and quick texture on buses — automate Amount into drops.

**Manual:** https://www.ujam.com/fileadmin/products/Manuals/FIN-MICRO_User_Guide.pdf

## UJAM Finisher RETRO (binary: FIN-RETRO)

**What it is:** Paid Finisher edition themed on vintage gear ("Vintage. Vibey. Cool.", $59).

**Main controls:** **50 modes organized by decade (60s/70s/80s/90s)**; one large **Finisher** macro knob (wet/dry-like but wired to many parameters) plus **four Vari knobs** whose labels change per mode to show what they tweak. That's the full macro set — dozens of processors under the hood, five knobs on top.

**Use for house:** lo-fi/retro color for melodic-house pads, keys and vocal chops; automate the Finisher knob for section-to-section vibe changes.

**Manual:** https://support.ujam.com/hc/en-us (Finisher RETRO User Guide); product page: https://www.ujam.com/finisher/

## UJAM Virtual Bassist ROWDY 2 (binary: VB-ROWDY)

**What it is:** UJAM's virtual session bassist for aggressive, pick-played rock/punk bass — a phrase player (Player mode) and playable bass (Instrument mode), not a synth bass.

**Main controls** (official ROWDY 2.1 user guide + product listings): Style/phrase selection with key switches and real-time controls in both Player and Instrument modes; **Character** control (preset voicings); choice of **DI signal or 3 amps, each with its own overdrive and fuzz**; octave control; simple EQ and dynamics section; **Finisher with 15 bass-optimized modes + one Finisher knob** (automatable).

**Use for house:** occasional indie-dance/organic-house live-bass flavor; for tech-house sub duties your synths (Serum/Diva/Operator) are the better tool.

**Manual:** https://support.ujam.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407184575506-Virtual-Bassist-ROWDY-2-1-User-Guide

## Analog Obsession LOVEND (binary: LOVEND) — NOT UJAM

**What it is:** Free harmonic **low-end/bass enhancer** by Analog Obsession (Patreon-funded freeware). Not an emulation of specific hardware; uses a Pultec-style boost/cut trick around the low shelf. v3.0 (2026) rewrote the DSP and added AAX — it sounds different from v2, so old projects may shift.

**Main controls:** **Frequency** (continuous 60–180 Hz), **Boost** (up to +10 dB low-end lift), **Output** (±10 dB); plugin label = bypass; GUI resizable 50–200%; lightweight anti-aliasing, no oversampling.

**Use for house:** thicken a thin kick/bass fundamental fast; stock fallback: Saturator (low-boost curve) or EQ Eight + Roar.

**Manual:** https://www.patreon.com/analogobsession (downloads & info; no formal manual published)

## United Plugins Urban Puncher (binary: UrbanPuncher) — Soundevice Digital, NOT UJAM

**What it is:** One-knob drum saturation/dynamics "fattener" for hard-hitting drums (United Plugins, 2021).

**Main controls:** **Punch** main knob (combined transient treatment + spectral shaping), **Saturation** (transformer/tube-style), **Destroy** button (triples the saturation), wet/dry **Mix**.

**Use for house:** parallel-style punch on kick/clap bus in one move; stock fallback: Drum Buss.

**Manual:** https://unitedplugins.com/UrbanPuncher/

## reFX Nexus 5 (binary: Nexus)

**What it is:** reFX's flagship preset workstation/ROMpler (current version NEXUS5; your installed version may be 4 or 5 — check the splash). Not a from-scratch sound-design synth: you play and shape premium presets and expansions.

**Main controls** (official manual): **Librarian** (preset browser; `/House`-style search filters expansions); **4 Macro controls** assigned via the **Mod-Matrix (20 slots)**; **Filter Modifier** (filter + dedicated envelope per layer); **Amp Modifier** (volume envelope, pan, drive); **Arpeggiator** (119 presets); **Trance gate** (52 presets); effects chain (dozens of FX; since v4.5 the **Routing page** replaces the old Effects/Trancegate/Layers pages); NEXUS5 adds 8 generator types (Virtual Analog, Sampler, Wavetable, Time Stretcher, Retro Sampler, Grain, Cloud, FM) and a sample editor with slicing.

**Use for house:** instant pluck/stab/lead presets — layer under Serum/Diva and resample; filter-modifier + trance gate for classic pumping gates.

**Manual:** https://refx.com/nexus/manual/

## KV331 Audio SynthMaster One (binaries: SynthMaster One, SynthMaster1FX)

**What it is:** Wavetable synth with the SynthMaster engine in a simpler one-page layout.

**Main controls** (kv331audio.com): 2 stereo oscillators (basic waveforms + wavetables, user-wavetable import, up to 16 unison voices each), 2 sub-oscillators (5 connection modes incl. ring/AM/PM/FM), 2 zero-delay-feedback filters (Ladder, Diode Ladder, State Variable, Bite), 4 ADSR envelopes, 2 LFOs, arpeggiator/step sequencer (16/32 steps), 11 effect types on 6 insert slots + 2 global bus inserts, 1,250 factory presets.

**SynthMaster1FX (unverified):** KV331's site does not document a desktop "One FX" product; this binary appears to be the effect-plugin build of SynthMaster One installed alongside the instrument (the engine running as a MIDI-controlled FX — a capability KV331 officially documents for the iOS version). Confirm by loading it in Live's browser on an audio track.

**Use for house:** strong free-adjacent wavetable plucks/arps; the built-in arp + delay covers melodic-tech sequences without leaving the plugin.

**Manual:** https://www.kv331audio.com/synthmasterone.aspx (downloads section hosts the PDF user guide)

## u-he TyrellN6 (binary: TyrellN6) — FREE

**What it is:** Free u-he virtual-analog synth (concept by Amazona.de, engine by u-he); v3.0.0 (March 2025) with CLAP/AUv2/VST3/AAX.

**Main controls** (u-he.com): 2 oscillators + noise + ring modulator into a mixer with overdrive and filter feedback; **twin filter related to an early Diva model**; 2 loopable analog-style ADSRs (LFO-triggerable); 2 host-syncable LFOs (8 waveforms); small mod matrix; unison up to 8 voices; 3-mode chorus; 580+ presets; MIDI learn; resizable UI.

**Use for house:** classic analog bass/stab tone for zero dollars — the Diva-related filter gives it real warmth; fallback relationship: it's the "mini Diva" to your full Diva.

**Manual:** https://dl.u-he.com/manuals/plugins/tyrelln6/TyrellN6-user-guide.pdf

## KORG Collection M1 (binary: M1)

**What it is:** Korg's official software recreation of the 1988 M1 music workstation (the "house piano/organ" machine), part of the KORG Collection.

**Main controls** (korg.com): complete M1 engine with all programs; additions over the hardware — **filter resonance** (missing on the original), **VDA modulation**, effects expanded from 2 master FX to **18 insert effects** (incl. compressor), sound browser filterable by instrument/character, **EASY mode** exposing key parameters, full edit pages, compatibility with original M1 and iM1 (iPad) data. Legacy-edition coverage also cites the T-series PCM expansion with its programs and combinations (current bundle contents **unverified** — check in-app).

**Use for house:** THE M1 Piano and M1 Organ presets — instant 90s house authenticity for piano stabs and organ bass.

**Manual:** https://www.korg.com/us/products/software/kc_m1/

## Universal Audio PolyMAX Synth (binary: uaudio_polymax)

**What it is:** UA's native (UADx) virtual-analog polysynth inspired by classic '80s polys.

**Main controls** (UA manual + reviews): 2 multi-wave oscillators with continuous **Shape** control (triangle → saw → square → narrow pulse) and **Shape Mod** (bipolar env/LFO amount), coarse/fine tune, FM and sync; pink/white noise generator and source mixer; selectable **2-pole/4-pole multimode filter** with cutoff, resonance, and direct LFO/envelope mod shortcuts; filter and amp envelopes; LFO (routable to oscillators and filter); envelopes routable to osc/amp/filter. Classic, deliberately simple routing.

**Use for house:** lush analog-style pads and poly chords for melodic house — sidechain it to the kick and it sits instantly.

**Manual:** https://help.uaudio.com/hc/en-us/articles/10207494851220-PolyMAX-Synth-Manual

## Spitfire LABS (binary: LABS) — now under Splice INSTRUMENT

**What it is:** Spitfire Audio's famous free sampled-instrument series. As of 2025/2026, **labs.spitfireaudio.com redirects to Splice: "Splice INSTRUMENT is the new home for LABS."** The newer green LABS plugin (LABS 2.0+) still reads existing content; LABS 1.0 content installs via the Spitfire Audio App; some packs (LABS Wurli, Sleigh Bells) were discontinued and can't be imported. New LABS+ packs require a Splice INSTRUMENT or Creator/Creator+ plan.

**Main controls:** deliberately minimal — each loaded instrument exposes only a handful of controls (typically a large main dial plus a few preset-specific knobs/faders) **(exact per-instrument layout unverified; it varies by library)**.

**Use for house:** free organic textures — soft piano, pads, atmospheres — for melodic-house breakdowns.

**Manual:** https://splice.com/instrument (current home; Spitfire Audio App for legacy content)

## Splice INSTRUMENT / Splice Sounds Plugin / Splice Sounds Listener / Splice Bridge

**What they are (all Splice, each verified on splice.com/support.splice.com):**
- **Splice INSTRUMENT (binary: Splice INSTRUMENT):** Splice's playable-instrument plugin and subscription — the new home of LABS content plus monthly exclusive instruments; its **Craft** feature turns Splice samples into playable instruments. Freemium + paid tier.
- **Splice Sounds Plugin (binary: Splice Sounds):** in-DAW Splice sample browser (beta) — search, preview and drag Splice sounds without leaving Live.
- **Splice Sounds Listener (binary: Splice Sounds Listener):** auto-installed companion AU/VST3 for the Sounds Plugin's **Search with Sound**: load it on a track/bus/master, it captures 8 bars on play and Splice recommends tempo/key-matched samples. If it errors, check both plugins are current and macOS Local Network/firewall permissions for Live.
- **SpliceBridge (binary: SpliceBridge):** links the Splice desktop app to the DAW so sample previews play **through Live, time-stretched and pitch-shifted to your project's tempo and key** (regular/half/double-time), and pastes the sound with that processing applied. Load on a MIDI track; requires the Splice app open and a paid Splice plan (Sounds+/Creator/Creator+ — note: your Splice connector/features need an active subscription).

**Use for house:** Bridge + Listener are the fastest way to audition tops/vocals in-key at your 120–126 BPM before spending credits.

**Manual:** https://splice.com/blog/how-to-use-splice-bridge and https://support.splice.com/en/articles/13860589-how-to-use-splice-sounds-listener

## Splice Astra (binary: Astra) — Splice, not Impact Soundworks/Output

**What it is:** Splice's own software synthesizer (included with Splice subscriptions).

**Main controls** (Splice's official guide): two identical multi-engine oscillators, each switchable between **virtual analog, FM, granular, sampler and wavetable** synthesis; sub oscillator; noise generator; multimode filter; 2 envelopes; 2 LFOs; sample & hold; comprehensive effects engine.

**Use for house:** quick hybrid plucks and granular textures from Splice samples; overlaps Serum/Vital, so treat it as a sketch synth.

**Manual:** https://splice.com/blog/how-to-use-astra (official Splice guide)

## AIR Music Technology instruments (binaries: MiniGrand, Velvet, Hybrid, Structure, Jura)

All by AIR Music Technology (inMusic). User guides: https://www.airmusictech.com (User Guides section).

### AIR Mini Grand (binary: MiniGrand)
**What it is:** Compact acoustic grand piano instrument with **seven piano models**, re-pedaling technology, room simulation, equal/stretched tuning. **Use for house:** light piano stabs without big sample-library overhead. **Manual:** https://www.airmusictech.com (Mini Grand user guide)

### AIR Velvet (binary: Velvet)
**What it is:** Vintage electric piano instrument (Velvet 2): **Fender Rhodes Suitcase, Rhodes MK I & MK II, Wurlitzer 200A, Hohner Pianet-T** models, ~350 presets. **Main controls:** preamp with tube overdrive & compression, tremolo/autopan, vintage & reverb effects. **Use for house:** dusty Rhodes chords for deep/melodic house, straight into RC-20. **Manual:** https://www.airmusictech.com (Velvet user guide)

### AIR Hybrid 3 (binary: Hybrid)
**What it is:** AIR's analog + wavetable synthesizer (Hybrid 3.0), long bundled with AIEP packs and MPC. Multi-part architecture with wavetable and VA oscillators **(deeper control-set details unverified here — see manual)**. **Use for house:** workhorse poly pads/basses; Serum/Diva remain first choice. **Manual:** https://www.airmusictech.com (Hybrid 3 user guide)

### AIR Structure 2 (binary: Structure)
**What it is:** Multi-timbral **sampler platform** with a ~37 GB premium library (originally Pro Tools' flagship sampler). **Use for house:** playback of its library and sample programs; in your workflow, Simpler/Sampler or Kontakt usually win. **Manual:** https://www.airmusictech.com (Structure 2 user guide)

### AIR Jura (binary: Jura) — instrument, not a chorus FX
**What it is:** AIR's 2023 recreation of the **Roland Juno-60** polysynth. The scanned `Jura` binary is this instrument; no separate official "Jura Chorus" FX plugin was found in AIR's catalog **(a standalone Jura Chorus product: unverified/not found)** — the famous Juno chorus is a section inside Jura. **Main controls:** Classic (original-panel) and Expanded GUI views; authentic 6-voice mode plus Unison and Chord modes; DCO with modern + vintage noise types; VCF with added saturation and drive; second envelope (beyond the original); the Juno chorus; extra FX section with delay, reverb and 3-band EQ. **Use for house:** the definitive melodic-house pad/bass machine — Juno pads + chorus + sidechain is half the genre. **Manual:** https://www.airmusictech.com (Jura user guide)

## Venomode Mesa Lite (binary: Mesa Lite) — effect, not an AIR instrument

**What it is:** Free clipper from Venomode — **Mesa Lite is a single soft-clipper module version of Mesa**, their multi-mode loudness clipper (released Nov 2025; VST/VST3/AU/AAX, macOS/Windows/Linux). Full Mesa (£49) adds 5 clip modes (hard/soft/2 Enhancer modes), 3-band multiband clipping, Mid/Side & L/R modes, in/out LUFS metering with difference readout, up to 16x oversampling.

**Main controls (Lite):** the soft clipper module with its drive/level controls **(exact knob names unverified — see manual)**.

**Use for house:** clip kick/drum-bus peaks before the limiter for loudness without pumping; you also own KClip3/KNOCK/FreeClip for the same job.

**Manual:** https://www.venomode.com/mesa/ (manual linked on page)

## Lunacy Audio CUBE (binary: CUBE)

**What it is:** 3D vector-synthesis sample instrument: up to **8 sample layers sit on the corners of a 3D cube**, and a floating **orb** (vector joystick) blends their volumes by position.

**Main controls** (launch specs): orb draggable, modulatable, or attached to **Orbits** — 3D motion paths, DAW-syncable or free-running, 40+ included plus an Orbit editor; per-layer ADSR, filter, saturation, volume, stereo width, loop, arpeggiator and tuning; 13 FX modules; 4 LFOs; macro controls; modulation matrix; 100+ sampled instruments, 400+ presets.

**Use for house:** evolving breakdown pads and textures that move on their own — automate the orbit rate into the drop.

**Manual:** https://lunacy.audio (CUBE product page & docs)

## Klevgrand SyndtSphere (binary: SyndtSphere) — FREE

**What it is:** Free (with Klevgrand account) polyphonic "sound surfer" synth: instead of parameter editing you **rotate a sphere holding 70+ designed sound states**; positions between nodes morph/interpolate the underlying parameters. Current desktop version 2.0.1 (AU/VST/AAX, macOS/Windows; also iOS AUv3).

**Main controls:** the sphere (browse/morph), Pitch Bend Range, Glide, Legato, built-in ping-pong delay, volume slider, state save.

**Use for house:** fast one-knob(-sphere) pads and keys when you don't want to sound-design; morph-automate it for evolving breakdowns.

**Manual:** https://klevgrand.com/products/syndtsphere
